F.A.Q

Questions, answered.

What is brand promotion?

Brand promotion is the strategic use of messaging, content and promotional channels to increase awareness and help relevant audiences discover a brand.

Can you promote a new brand?

Yes. New and developing brands can be considered depending on their goals, content and promotion requirements.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. Promotion can improve awareness and reach, but sales depend on factors such as the offer, pricing, audience demand and market conditions.
